## Part I. Jurisdictional Context

### 1.1 The City of Fresno

The City of Fresno is the fifth most populous city in California with an estimated population of approximately 544,000 residents as of 2026 and operates as the county seat of Fresno County in the San Joaquin Valley. The City operates under a strong-mayor council-manager hybrid charter form of government with the Mayor elected at large, seven City Council members elected by district, and a City Manager, City Attorney, and City Clerk appointed under the specific framework of the City Charter.

### 1.2 Scope of This Audit

This audit covers the Non-Sunshine Universe (NSU) of the City of Fresno — the universe of City records available for analytical ingest outside the specific records already captured in the Fresno County MAS V4.0 (completed July 31, 2026). The NSU scope includes records produced through the City Legistar harvest: City Council agendas, minutes, resolutions, ordinances, staff reports, agreements, contracts, amendments, MOUs, procurement records, grant documents, fiscal records, and administrative records.

### 1.3 Relationship to the Fresno Metropolitan MAS Series

This Fresno City MAS V2 is the successor to the Fresno Metropolitan MAS V3.0 (September 2026) specifically for the City of Fresno component. The MAS V2 designation reflects that this is the second-generation MAS for the City of Fresno specifically. The MAS V2 incorporates the complete NSU pattern substrate and operates as the specific analytical foundation for the Fresno Metropolitan MAS V4.0 successor, which will integrate the City of Fresno MAS V2 with the Fresno County MAS V4.0 and the FPD MAS V4.0 into a unified Metropolitan successor document.

## Part II. Methodology (O.D.I.A. and R.A.I.A.)

### 2.1 Platform Methodology Stack

This audit operates under the O.D.I.A. platform at version 3.9.2 using the R.A.I.A. methodology as the second-layer analytical framework. O.D.I.A. operates on the twenty-layer detector suite (L-1 through L-20 across the odia_legal and C.O.N.T.R.A. detector series plus the broader anomaly detector layers), the FastAPI backend with over fifty endpoints, the Next.js 14 frontend, the Electron desktop app, the odia-v1 RAG pipeline on Ollama with TF-IDF retrieval, and the SQLite database.

### 2.2 R.A.I.A. (Recursive Analysis Investigative Audit)

R.A.I.A. is the second-layer methodology that iteratively re-examines, cross-references, pattern-synthesizes, and escalates O.D.I.A. detector output to specific investigative conclusions. The R.A.I.A. methodology operative in this MAS V2 includes:

1. Layer-by-layer finding extraction from the twenty-two operational detector layers
2. Cross-layer pattern synthesis identifying intersections that produce higher-confidence signals
3. Dollar-weighted and severity-weighted aggregation producing headline exposures
4. Per-pattern doctrinal mapping against operative statutory, regulatory, and constitutional frameworks
5. Specific litigation and policy application identification for each pattern category
6. Specific document prioritization for subsequent per-document review

### 2.3 Severity Framework

| Level | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Specific compliance failures of substantial dollar, constitutional, or doctrinal weight warranting individual review |
| **High** | Specific compliance failures warranting pattern-level synthesis and accountability engagement |
| **Medium** | Specific compliance patterns warranting aggregate analysis and research track development |
| **Low** | Baseline statutory implication; denominator against which higher-severity findings calibrate |

### 2.4 Detector Calibration Transparency

- **L-8 Case Law Currency**: Produced exactly 1 finding across the entire corpus — substantial undercalibration pending odia_legal Phase 3 CourtListener integration
- **Constitutional layer**: Produced exactly 1 finding — constitutional analysis substrate distributed across L-6 and L-10
