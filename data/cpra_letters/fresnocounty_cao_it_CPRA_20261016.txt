Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (CPRA), Government Code §§ 7920.000
et seq., I respectfully request the following public records. This request is
directed to the County Administrative Officer and the Department of Information
Technology and Services (ITS) as custodians of county-wide technology governance

32,340 legislative documents — identifying surveillance infrastructure and AI
procurement activities lacking required governance documentation under California
law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 1 — SB 524 AI-TRANSPARENCY IMPACT ASSESSMENTS (COUNTY-WIDE)
           [SB 524 / Gov. Code § 11548.05 — all County AI procurements]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SB 524 (Stats. 2023, ch. 833) requires agencies deploying automated decision
systems or AI-assisted tools to complete an AI-transparency impact assessment
prior to procurement. BOS records identified multiple County departments
procuring or operating AI-assisted surveillance and law enforcement tools —
including ALPR systems, AI report-writing software, and facial recognition —
without citation to a completed SB 524 assessment.

Please provide:

  1a. All SB 524 AI-transparency impact assessments or equivalent pre-procurement
      AI risk assessments completed by any Fresno County department or ITS for
      any AI-assisted system, automated decision tool, or algorithmic platform,
      from January 1, 2020 through the date of this request.

  1b. The County's enterprise AI governance policy, AI procurement checklist,
      or technology review framework, as currently in effect, governing County
      departments' acquisition and deployment of AI or automated decision systems.

  1c. A list of all AI-assisted or automated decision systems currently operated
      by or on behalf of Fresno County, including vendor name, system purpose,
      department owner, and year of deployment.

  1d. Any ITS or CAO directives, memoranda, or guidance issued to County
      departments on AI governance, SB 524 compliance, or automated systems
      review procedures.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 2 — COUNTY-WIDE SURVEILLANCE INFRASTRUCTURE POLICY
           [132 docs without transparency portal; 129 without data-retention policy]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOS records identified 132 documents referencing County surveillance capabilities
without citation to a public surveillance transparency portal or inventory, and
129 without citation to a data-retention policy for surveillance data. County ITS
typically maintains enterprise data governance policies applicable across departments.

Please provide:

  2a. The County's enterprise data retention policy, including retention schedules
      for surveillance data (ALPR reads, BWC footage, drone footage, facial
      recognition records) as applied to all County departments.

  2b. The County's public surveillance transparency report, technology inventory,
      or online registry of active surveillance systems, as required or voluntarily
      published under AB 481 or the County's own technology governance framework.

  2c. ITS's current network and data security policies applicable to law
      enforcement surveillance data stored on County systems or transmitted
      to or from cloud vendors (including Axon Evidence.com, Flock Safety,
      and any regional data-sharing networks).

  2d. Any cross-departmental MOUs governing access to County surveillance
      data by non-County entities, including federal agencies, regional
      task forces, or private vendors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 3 — AUTO-RENEWAL CONTRACTS AND ENTERPRISE TECHNOLOGY AGREEMENTS
           [303 documents with auto-renewal clauses]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Three hundred and three documents in BOS records reflect contracts containing
auto-renewal clauses that renew without an affirmative Board vote unless timely
notice is given. ITS and the CAO typically manage enterprise-level technology
master agreements subject to this risk.

Please provide:

  3a. All enterprise-level technology master agreements (ELAs, MSAs, SaaS
      subscriptions, and cloud service agreements) currently in effect between
      the County and any technology vendor, including Axon, Motorola Solutions,
      Flock Safety, T-Mobile, Microsoft, Oracle, Tyler Technologies, and similar
      — together with the auto-renewal provision (if any) in each.

  3b. The County's current policy requiring department heads or ITS to notify
      the CAO and the BOS prior to an auto-renewal taking effect on any contract
      above the County's competitive bidding threshold.

  3c. Any master contracts or enterprise agreements that have auto-renewed without
      a BOS reauthorization vote in the last five fiscal years, together with
      the CAO notification (or explanation of its absence) for each.

REQUEST 4 — T-MOBILE / TELECOMMUNICATIONS CONTRACTS
           [13 documents with T-Mobile reference]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Thirteen documents in BOS records reference T-Mobile (or related T-Mobile
Government entities) as a telecommunications vendor for County law enforcement
or public safety communications infrastructure.

Please provide:

  4a. All contracts, service orders, and data-sharing agreements with T-Mobile
      (including FirstNet/AT&T comparators) for County public safety or law
      enforcement telecommunications services, from January 1, 2018 through
      the date of this request.

  4b. Any County contracts or MOUs that allow T-Mobile or its subcontractors

  4c. The County's current policy on law enforcement access to carrier-provided
      location data, including any cell-site simulator or stingray devices
      operated by or accessible to County departments.
