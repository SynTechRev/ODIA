Dear City Clerk:

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (CPRA), Government Code §§ 7920.000
et seq., I respectfully request the following public records. This request arises
from a review of City of Fresno Council agenda packets, staff reports, and
attachments — a corpus spanning multiple fiscal years — in which 2,956 or more
documents were identified as containing high-dollar financial instruments lacking
valid or complete signature elements consistent with the City's own contract
execution requirements and California public contracting law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 1 — UNSIGNED OR INSUFFICIENTLY EXECUTED CONTRACTS AND AGREEMENTS
           [2,956 documents identified — CRITICAL priority]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A systematic review of City Council legislative records identified 2,956 or more
documents referencing financial instruments — including contracts, professional
services agreements, memoranda of understanding, grant agreements, sole-source
authorizations, and capital expenditure agreements — in which signature execution
appeared absent, incomplete, or unverifiable from the public record. Sample
instruments include transactions valued at $10,024,426.24 and $3,100,000.

Under California Public Contract Code §§ 20160–20175, Government Code § 37350,
and the City's own contract execution policy, all contracts obligating public
funds above applicable thresholds require proper authorization, execution, and
public record. Instruments that bypass or omit required signature execution create
unenforceable obligations, risk fiscal exposure, and undermine public accountability.

Please provide:

  1a. All contracts, professional services agreements, memoranda of understanding
      (MOUs), and grant agreements executed by or on behalf of the City of Fresno
      in which the final, fully executed version bearing all required signatures
      (City Manager, Mayor, or authorized department head, as applicable, plus
      counterparty execution) has NOT been posted to the City's public records
      portal or Legistar system, for fiscal years 2020-21 through 2025-26.

  1b. All contracts, agreements, or instruments valued at $500,000 or above —
      including amendments and change orders — for which the public record reflects
      Council approval but does not contain a complete, fully executed copy,
      for the same period.

  1c. The City's current contract execution policy and signature authorization
      matrix, including delegated signing authority thresholds for the City Manager,
      department directors, and any authorized designees.

  1d. The City's policy and procedure for posting fully executed contracts to the
      public portal — including the required timeline between Council approval and
      posting of the executed instrument.

  1e. Any City Controller, City Auditor, or Internal Audit Division reports,
      findings, or management letters concerning contract execution compliance,
      signature gaps, or incomplete procurement records, for fiscal years
      2020-21 through 2025-26.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 2 — SOLE-SOURCE AND EMERGENCY CONTRACT EXECUTION RECORDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Among the unsigned or incompletely executed instruments identified, a subset
involved sole-source procurement justifications or emergency authorization. For
these instruments:

  2a. All sole-source authorization memoranda, emergency contract certifications,
      and waiver-of-competitive-bidding findings approved by the City Manager or
      City Council from fiscal years 2020-21 through 2025-26, along with the
      corresponding fully executed contract for each.

  2b. All competitive bid waivers, piggyback authorizations, and cooperative
      purchasing agreements (including NASPO, OMNIA, and similar cooperative
      vehicles) used by the City of Fresno, together with the executed agreement
      for each, for the same period.

  2c. The City's protest and dispute resolution records related to any procurement
      where a contract award was challenged on signature or execution grounds,
      for the same period.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 3 — GRANT AGREEMENTS AND FEDERAL FUND INSTRUMENT EXECUTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Federal and state grant agreements require co-execution by authorized officials
and are subject to the Uniform Guidance (2 CFR Part 200) requirements for
internal controls over federal expenditures (2 CFR § 200.303). For grant
instruments:

  3a. All fully executed federal and state grant agreements, sub-award agreements,
      and cooperative agreements under which the City of Fresno receives or
      administers federal or state funds, for grant years 2020 through 2026, where
      the executed instrument is not publicly accessible via the City's online
      portal or Legistar.

  3b. For any federal grant instrument not bearing a complete co-execution record
      in the public file: the grant application, award notification, and
      City Council resolution accepting the award — together with any audit or
      monitoring findings referencing incomplete or missing execution.

  3c. The City's written policies and procedures for internal controls over
      federal award execution per 2 CFR § 200.303, as most recently updated.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 4 — CAPITAL PROJECT AND PUBLIC WORKS CONTRACT EXECUTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Capital improvement projects and public works contracts carry heightened execution
requirements under California Public Contract Code §§ 20160–20175 (formal bidding),
§ 22032 (informal bidding under CUPCCAA), and the City's own Capital Improvement
Program policy.

  4a. All public works contracts and design-build agreements approved by City
      Council for projects valued at $500,000 or above, for fiscal years 2020-21
      through 2025-26, along with the fully executed contract (including contractor
      and City signatures, performance and payment bond documentation) for each.

  4b. Any Capital Improvement Project contract for which the public file reflects
      a Council award but does not contain: (i) the final executed contract,
      (ii) the contractor's performance and payment bonds, or (iii) the Notice
      to Proceed — for the same period.

  4c. The City's list of any contracts currently under dispute, litigation, or
      claim where the dispute involves signature authority, execution validity,
      or unauthorized contract modifications, for fiscal years 2020-21 through
      2025-26.
