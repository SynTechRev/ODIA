Dear Clerk of the Board:

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (CPRA), Government Code §§ 7920.000
et seq., I respectfully request the following public records. This request arises
from a review of Fresno County Board of Supervisors agenda packets, staff reports,
and attachments — a corpus of 32,340 legislative documents — in which significant
and recurring compliance gaps were identified in contract execution, federal grant
certification, and procurement governance.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 1 — UNSIGNED OR INCOMPLETELY EXECUTED CONTRACTS AND AGREEMENTS
           [4,120 documents identified — CRITICAL priority, ~$14.97B total value]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A systematic review of Fresno County BOS legislative records identified 4,120 or
more documents referencing financial instruments — including contracts, professional
services agreements, memoranda of understanding, grant agreements, sole-source
authorizations, and capital expenditure instruments — in which signature execution
appeared absent, incomplete, or unverifiable from the public record. The aggregate
value of these instruments is approximately $14.97 billion across fiscal years
reviewed.

Under California Public Contract Code §§ 20160–20175, Government Code § 25502.5,
and the County's own contract execution policies, all contracts obligating public
funds require proper authorization, co-execution by both parties, and posting to
the public record.

Please provide:

  1a. All contracts, professional services agreements, and MOUs executed by or
      on behalf of Fresno County in which the final, fully executed version
      bearing all required signatures (Board Chair or CAO, as applicable, plus
      counterparty) has NOT been posted to the Board's public Legistar or records
      portal, for fiscal years 2019-20 through 2025-26.

  1b. All contracts, agreements, or instruments valued at $500,000 or above —
      including amendments and change orders — for which the public record reflects
      Board approval but does not contain a complete, fully executed copy,
      for the same period.

  1c. The County's current contract execution policy and signature authorization
      matrix, including delegated signing authority thresholds for the CAO,
      department directors, and any authorized designees.

  1d. Any County Auditor-Controller, Internal Audit, or Grand Jury reports,
      findings, or management letters concerning contract execution compliance,
      signature gaps, or incomplete procurement records, for fiscal years
      2019-20 through 2025-26.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 2 — JAG/EDWARD BYRNE AND COPS GRANT COMPLIANCE
           [190 critical + 35 high-severity documents]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A review of BOS records identified 190 or more documents referencing Edward Byrne
Memorial Justice Assistance Grant (JAG) funds awarded to or administered through
Fresno County without the anti-supplanting certification required under 34 U.S.C.
§ 10152(a)(1)(G). An additional 14 documents reference COPS grants without the
required technology cost itemization. Two documents reference criminal intelligence
activities without citation to 28 CFR Part 23 compliance requirements.

Please provide:

  2a. All JAG award letters, sub-award agreements, and MOUs accepted by or flowing
      through Fresno County from the U.S. Department of Justice / BJA for grant
      years 2006 through 2026, including allocations to the Sheriff's Department,
      Probation Department, and any sub-recipient agencies.

  2b. The anti-supplanting certification submitted with each JAG application or
      award acceptance for the same period.

  2c. All JAG programmatic and financial progress reports (SF-PPR and SF-425
      or equivalent) submitted to BJA for grant years 2006 through 2026.

  2d. All COPS grant awards, technology purchase plans, and annual progress
      reports for grant years 2010 through 2026.

  2e. All BOS resolutions and agenda items authorizing JAG or COPS grant
      acceptance, technology expenditures, and compliance reporting.

  2f. Any BJA, OIG, or Grand Jury findings concerning Fresno County JAG or COPS
      compliance, anti-supplanting, or technology procurement.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 3 — SOLE-SOURCE AND PROCUREMENT GOVERNANCE
           [1,765 high-severity sole-source documents; 303 auto-renewal docs]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A review of BOS records identified 1,765 documents referencing sole-source or
no-bid procurement without citation to a Government Code § 10340 justification
finding, and 303 documents in which vendor contracts appear to include automatic
renewal clauses that renew without an affirmative BOS vote unless notice is given —
creating ongoing fiscal obligations without required legislative reauthorization.

Please provide:

  3a. All sole-source authorization memoranda, emergency contract certifications,
      and findings of fact supporting a waiver of competitive bidding requirements
      (Gov. Code § 10340), approved by the Board of Supervisors or CAO, for
      fiscal years 2019-20 through 2025-26.

  3b. All contracts containing automatic renewal clauses currently in effect that
      have renewed without an affirmative BOS vote or have been authorized for
      renewal through a consent calendar item rather than a separately noticed
      agenda item, for fiscal years 2019-20 through 2025-26.

  3c. The County's current policy on auto-renewal clauses and the required
      notification and reauthorization process before an auto-renewal takes effect.

  3d. All piggyback authorizations and cooperative purchasing agreements
      (NASPO, OMNIA, and similar) executed by the County, for the same period.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 4 — SURVEILLANCE TECHNOLOGY WITHOUT BOARD AUTHORIZATION
           [92 critical governance documents]
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Ninety-two documents in BOS records reference County department surveillance
capabilities — including ALPR systems, body-worn cameras, drones/UAS, and facial
recognition — without any corresponding BOS resolution, agenda authorization, or
public hearing record, in potential conflict with AB 481 (Gov. Code § 7070 et seq.)
and California's general requirement of legislative authorization for county
department equipment acquisitions above applicable thresholds.

Please provide:

  4a. All BOS resolutions, agenda items, and staff reports authorizing Fresno
      County departments to acquire or operate any surveillance technology —
      including ALPR systems, body-worn cameras, drones/UAS, facial recognition
      systems, or predictive policing tools — from January 1, 2018 through
      the date of this request.

  4b. The County's AB 481 Militarized Equipment Use Policy, all annual reports
      submitted to the BOS under AB 481, and all BOS resolutions approving or
      renewing that policy, for fiscal years 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25,
      and 2025-26.

  4c. The AB 481 equipment inventory for each year referenced above.

  4d. All surveillance-related items that have appeared on a BOS consent calendar
      (rather than discussion agenda) since January 1, 2018, together with the
      BOS minutes for those meetings.
