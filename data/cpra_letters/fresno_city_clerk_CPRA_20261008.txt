Dear City Clerk:

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (CPRA), as recodified at Government
Code §§ 7920.000 et seq., I respectfully request the following public records. These
requests arise from a pattern of surveillance technology deployments identified in
City of Fresno legislative records that lack the statutory compliance documentation
required under current California law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 1 — FLOCK SAFETY ALPR SYSTEM (Civil Code § 1798.90.5 / SB 524)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A review of City Council agenda packets, staff reports, and attachments (2020–2026)
identified 25 or more documents referencing Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR)
deployment in the City of Fresno without any citation to the agency's Civil Code
§ 1798.90.5 ALPR usage and privacy policy, and without the SB 524 AI-transparency
impact assessment required before operational use.

Please provide:

  1a. All contracts, purchase orders, amendments, and renewal options between the
      City of Fresno (including Fresno Police Department) and Flock Safety, Inc.,
      for ALPR system procurement, installation, maintenance, or data services,
      for the period January 1, 2018 through the date of this request.

  1b. The City's current ALPR usage and privacy policy required by Civil Code
      § 1798.90.5, including any version history, adoption resolutions, and the
      date the policy was last reviewed or updated.

  1c. All SB 524 (Stats. 2023, ch. 833) AI-transparency impact assessments or
      equivalent documentation prepared in connection with any ALPR system or
      AI-assisted tool deployed by the Fresno Police Department.

  1d. All City Council agenda items, staff reports, resolutions, and minutes
      related to ALPR system authorization, funding, or renewal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 2 — AXON ENTERPRISE (Draft One / AI Report Writing) (SB 524)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Multiple city agenda documents reference AI-assisted police report writing
(including Axon's "Draft One" or equivalent product) without any citation to an
SB 524 AI-transparency impact assessment.

Please provide:

  2a. All contracts, purchase orders, and amendments between the City of Fresno
      or Fresno Police Department and Axon Enterprise, Inc. (including body-worn
      camera hardware, software subscriptions, AI analytics, and Draft One),
      for the period January 1, 2018 through the date of this request.

  2b. All SB 524 (Stats. 2023, ch. 833) impact assessments, AI use policy
      documentation, or equivalent compliance records prepared in connection
      with AI-assisted report writing tools deployed by FPD.

  2c. All City Council approvals, budget line items, and any sole-source
      justifications for Axon Enterprise contracts exceeding $50,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 3 — AB 481 ANNUAL MILITARIZED EQUIPMENT REPORTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Seven documents in City Council records reference Fresno Police Department drone
and UAS programs without AB 481 (Stats. 2021, ch. 825) annual-report language.

Please provide:

  3a. The Fresno Police Department's AB 481 Militarized Equipment Use Policy,
      all annual reports submitted to the City Council under AB 481 (§ 7072),
      and all Council resolutions approving or renewing that policy, for
      fiscal years 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, and 2025-26.

  3b. The equipment inventory disclosed in each AB 481 annual report,
      including drones/UAS, ALPR systems, less-lethal weapons, and any
      surveillance equipment acquired with federal grants.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 4 — FACIAL RECOGNITION (AB 1215 COMPLIANCE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Eleven documents in City Council records reference facial recognition technology,
subject to the restrictions in AB 1215 (Stats. 2019, ch. 783).

Please provide:

  4a. All records reflecting the City's or FPD's use of, or access to, facial
      recognition technology from January 1, 2019 through the date of this
      request, including any contracts, data-sharing agreements, and any
      reports or incidents involving facial recognition output used in
      criminal investigations.

  4b. All AB 1215 compliance documentation, legal opinions, or policy
      determinations regarding facial recognition use or moratorium status.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 5 — JAG GRANT COMPLIANCE DOCUMENTATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Please provide:

  5a. All Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant (JAG) award letters,
      sub-award agreements, and compliance certifications accepted by the City
      of Fresno or Fresno Police Department for grant years 2019 through 2026.

  5b. All equipment purchases made with JAG funds, including any surveillance
      technology, ALPR, body cameras, or related hardware.

  5c. All required JAG programmatic and financial reports submitted to the
      Bureau of Justice Assistance (BJA) for grant years 2019 through 2026.
