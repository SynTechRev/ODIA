Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (CPRA), Government Code §§ 7920.000
et seq., I respectfully request the following public records from the Fresno Police
Department. This request addresses operational use of surveillance and AI-assisted
policing technologies for which statutory compliance documentation could not be
located in publicly available Council records.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 1 — FLOCK SAFETY ALPR OPERATIONAL RECORDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1a. The FPD operational policy governing use of Flock Safety ALPR data,
      including data retention schedules, access controls, and audit log
      requirements, as currently in effect.

  1b. All data-sharing agreements between FPD and any other agency (federal,
      state, county, or municipal) regarding Flock Safety ALPR data, including
      any agreements granting Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) or
      federal law enforcement access to ALPR data or queries.

  1c. Statistical reports or dashboards reflecting ALPR query volume, plate-read
      volume, and any "hot list" alert volume for calendar years 2023, 2024,
      and 2025.

  1d. The Civil Code § 1798.90.5 ALPR usage and privacy policy, including the
      date it was adopted, any annual updates, and the public-facing location
      where it is posted.

  1e. Records of any audits, internal affairs investigations, or civilian
      complaints related to ALPR system misuse or unauthorized access,
      from January 1, 2020 through the date of this request (with personally
      identifying information redacted as authorized under Gov. Code § 7923.600).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 2 — AXON DRAFT ONE / AI REPORT WRITING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  2a. All FPD policies, procedures, or directives governing officer use of
      Axon Draft One (AI-generated narrative report writing), including any
      supervisor review requirements and documentation of officer training.

  2b. The SB 524 (Stats. 2023, ch. 833) AI-transparency impact assessment, or
      equivalent documentation, prepared before FPD deployed any AI-assisted
      report-writing tool.

  2c. Any records reflecting the number of reports generated with AI assistance
      vs. officer-authored reports, for the most recent 12-month period for
      which data is available.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 3 — BODY-WORN CAMERA PROGRAM (CJIS COMPLIANCE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  3a. The FPD body-worn camera policy as currently in effect, including data
      retention, access controls, evidentiary upload procedures, and any
      specific reference to CJIS Security Policy compliance.

  3b. The most recent CJIS Security Policy compliance documentation or audit
      results applicable to FPD's BWC data storage and access systems.

  3c. The Axon BWC contract in effect as of the date of this request, including
      cloud storage terms, data sovereignty provisions, and any federal law
      enforcement access provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 4 — DRONE / UAS PROGRAM (AB 481)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  4a. FPD's current drone/UAS Militarized Equipment Use Policy adopted pursuant
      to AB 481 (Gov. Code § 7070 et seq.).

  4b. All AB 481 annual reports submitted to the City Council for fiscal years
      2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, and 2025-26.

  4c. All FAA authorizations (Certificates of Authorization or Part 107 waivers)
      obtained by FPD for drone operations.

  4d. Any incidents or uses of drones in connection with protests, First
      Amendment-protected activities, or civil disturbances, from January 1,
      2020 through the date of this request.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REQUEST 5 — FACIAL RECOGNITION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  5a. All records reflecting FPD use of facial recognition technology (including
      any use of external services such as Clearview AI, Amazon Rekognition,
      or equivalent) from January 1, 2019 through the date of this request.

  5b. Any FPD facial recognition policy, legal opinion on AB 1215 applicability,
      or documentation of compliance with the moratorium period (2019–2023).

  5c. Any investigative leads or arrests in which facial recognition output
      played a material role, for the period covered by this request (with
      case-specific identifying information redacted as authorized).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
